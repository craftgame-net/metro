# Metro

Metro is a minecart metro on ordinary rails. A rider presses a button or steps
on a plate at a platform, picks a station in a menu, and the plugin drives the
cart there. The rider never steers, and never changes carts between connected lines.

The plugin is made for one shared metro for the whole server, built and kept
up by trusted players who know what they are doing.
